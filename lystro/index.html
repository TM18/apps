Lystro

A clean, fast shopping list app. Create lists, add items, attach photos, and set reminders - everything stays privately on your device.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play
Lystro is designed with your privacy in mind. Your shopping lists, items, and photos are stored exclusively on your device - never uploaded, never shared.

Features

📋

Shopping Lists

Create, complete, and duplicate lists. Track progress with item counts.

📷

Photo Attachments

Attach photos to any item using your camera or photo library.

🔔

Reminders

Set a date and time reminder for any list. Delivered as local notifications.

🌍

33 Languages

Full interface and calendar localisation for 33 languages.

🎨

Themes & Colors

Light, dark, or system theme. Eight accent colors to choose from.

🔒

Private by Design

All data lives on your device. No accounts, no sync, no backend.
